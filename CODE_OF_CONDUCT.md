# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Welcome contributors with different experience levels and backgrounds. Discuss technical disagreements using reproducible evidence. Harassment, discriminatory conduct, threats, publication of private information and deliberate credential exposure are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation to protect contributors. Report concerns privately using the contact route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be handled with discretion.
